# Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer

Spec: [design-spec](../spec/design-spec.md)

## 1. Purpose and Layer Boundary

`Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer` is the use-case orchestration and streaming coordination layer of the File Transfer plugin (`AppId: 0x03`). It connects the pure bundle geometry and state machines of [`Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer.Domain`](../Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer.Domain/Design.md) with [`Percolator.PluginSdk`](../Percolator.PluginSdk/Design.md) to coordinate in-band control negotiation alongside out-of-band multi-stream bulk chunk transfer.

- **In Scope:** In-band offer/accept/peer-join/cancel and E2E ICE signaling negotiation over Double Ratchet (1:1) and Group V2 SenderKey (group), edge ingress policy filtering, Group V2 member-eviction certificate revocation, adaptive chunk sizing and inline-vs-blob manifest distribution, pipelined rarest-first multi-stream chunk scheduling, verify-before-write disk assembly, unlocked offline-author seeding lifecycle, and blinded swarm presence coordination.
- **Out of Scope:** Pure bundle math and Merkle tree algebra (`Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer.Domain`), host messaging ratchets, Protobuf `.proto` compilation, physical OS file handles, sparse file ioctls, `DataChannelDotnet` / `libdatachannel` native handles, UDP STUN sockets, TLS 1.3 `TransferPipe` streams, and SQLite storage (`Percolator.Infrastructure`).

## 2. Architectural Invariants and Encapsulation

1. **`INV-VERIFY-BEFORE-WRITE` (Verify-Before-Write Disk Hygiene):** Every inbound chunk must arrive over an E2E-pinned mutually authenticated transport (DTLS or optional TLS 1.3 mTLS pipe) and pass domain `MerkleTree` SHA-256 leaf verification in memory (per [`DEC-DOMAIN-SEPARATED-MERKLE`](../Percolator.Apps.FileTransfer.Domain/Design.md#3-key-technical-design-choices)) *before* any byte slice is written via `IDesktopFileStorage`. Unauthenticated or corrupted chunks immediately choke the offending peer and drop the stream with zero disk writes.
2. **`INV-POOLED-STREAM-BUFFERS` (Zero-Allocation Pooled Streaming Path):** Chunk reads, binary framing, and Merkle hashing rent and return buffers from `ArrayPool<byte>.Shared` to prevent Large Object Heap (LOH) fragmentation during multi-stream saturation.
3. **`INV-SILENT-OFFER-STAGING` (Silent Pre-Consent Staging — Anti-Presence-Probing):** Inbound transfer offers are filtered at the edge and staged locally in `PendingConsent` without emitting any automatic network receipt, opening transfer sockets, or probing peer endpoints until the user explicitly accepts.
4. **`INV-EPHEMERAL-SESSION-LISTENER` (On-Demand Transfer-Scoped Sockets & Atomic Lock Teardown):** Zero listening sockets or ICE agents exist at startup, while idle, or during chat/profile traffic (realizing [`SYS-PRE-ACTIVATION-STEALTH`](../SystemRequirements.md#2-security-privacy-and-risk-control-invariants) and [`SYS-FILETRANSFER-DIRECT-DATAPLANE-AND-DISCLOSURE`](../SystemRequirements.md#2-security-privacy-and-risk-control-invariants)). Ephemeral ICE agents, DataChannels, and optional fallback pipes are owned strictly by the persona-scoped `ITransferSessionRuntime` (`IAsyncDisposable`), instantiated exclusively during user-consented Send/Accept or unlocked seeding, and torn down atomically alongside `SwarmTrackerService` unregistration upon manual stop, Group V2 member removal, or vault lock (realizing [`SYS-LOCK-TEARDOWN-AND-ZEROIZATION`](../SystemRequirements.md#2-security-privacy-and-risk-control-invariants)). `#session-lock`
5. **`INV-STANDARD-CRYPTO-ONLY` (Zero Custom Data-Plane Cryptography):** Bulk transfer confidentiality and authentication rely exclusively on standard transport cryptography (DTLS over ICE DataChannels or `.NET` `SslStream` TLS 1.3 mTLS over optional `TransferPipe`) with 32-byte SHA-256 certificate fingerprints pinned solely over Double Ratchet or Group V2 SenderKey, paired with standard SHA-256 Merkle chunk verification and CSPRNG 32-byte `SwarmRendezvousId` values (implementing [`DEC-FILETRANSFER-DATAPLANE-TRANSPORT`](../DecisionRegister.md#3-integration-transport-and-platform-decisions)). Hand-rolled chunk AEAD ciphers, deterministic `BaseNonce XOR ChunkIndex` schemes, shared swarm encryption keys, and custom rendezvous KDFs are strictly prohibited.

## 3. Key Technical Design Choices

1. **`DEC-DUAL-PLANE-TRANSFER` (Dual-Plane Bifurcation — E2E Control/Signaling vs. Pinned Standard Data Plane):**
   - *Design Choice:* Realizes [`DEC-FILETRANSFER-DATAPLANE-TRANSPORT`](../DecisionRegister.md#3-integration-transport-and-platform-decisions). Lightweight control frames (`FileTransferOffer`, `FileTransferAccept`, `FileTransferPeerJoin`, `FileTransferCancel`, and E2E SDP/ICE signaling carrying each participant's 32-byte SHA-256 certificate fingerprint) travel over Double Ratchet (1:1) or Group V2 SenderKey (group), whereas bulk file chunks stream over out-of-band `DataChannelDotnet` ICE/DTLS/SCTP DataChannels (or optional operator-opt-in zero-disk TLS 1.3 mTLS `TransferPipe` fallback) authenticated via E2E-pinned certificate fingerprints and constant-time session tokens. Completed downloads transition to `Seeding` and continue seeding while the app stays unlocked so Group V2 swarms survive when the original author goes offline, while Group V2 `MemberLeft` (`Epoch E -> E+1`) immediately revokes evicted peers' pinned fingerprints and drops their data channels. `#bitfield-swarm`
   - *Rejected Alternative:* Streaming bulk file chunks through the Double Ratchet pipeline (causing head-of-line blocking for chat) or retaining legacy custom deterministic chunk AEAD (`ChunkCryptoService` / `BaseNonce XOR ChunkIndex`) over raw outbound TCP.
2. **`DEC-TWO-TIER-MANIFEST` (Two-Tier Manifest Distribution — Inline vs. Out-of-Band Blob):**
   - Small manifests travel inline inside the offer control frame for single-roundtrip startup, while large multi-file manifests are uploaded once via `IMediaBlobService` (per [`DEC-OOB-BLOB-REFERENCE`](../Percolator.PluginSdk/Design.md#3-key-technical-design-choices)) with a bounded stream reader on download to prevent memory-exhaustion bombs.
3. **`DEC-PRIVACY-GATED-PEX` (Blinded Zero-Disk Swarm Presence & E2E-Only Candidate Signaling):**
   - Online swarm peer presence is coordinated via an unauthenticated, RAM-only Exchange `SwarmTrackerService` keyed strictly by random 32-byte `SwarmRendezvousId` and session-scoped `EphemeralSwarmPeerId` (never transmitting Persona IDs, certificate fingerprints, or LAN IPs to the Exchange), while all ICE candidates and certificate fingerprints travel exclusively inside E2E Double Ratchet or Group V2 envelopes and are restricted to approved contacts or authorized group members. `#relay-privacy`
